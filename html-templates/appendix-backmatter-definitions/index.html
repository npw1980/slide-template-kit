Appendix C — definitions

Terms used throughout the staffing study and exactly how each is measured

Term Definition
Span of Control (SoC)
  • Number of people reporting directly to one individual; the ratio of managers to staff in an organization
  • Example: a manager who directly manages 5 employees has a SoC ratio of 5:1
Management Layer
  • Number of organizational levels having supervisory responsibilities
  • Also referred to as “management levels”; the taxonomy recognizes three layers
Activity Analysis
  • Survey of effort expended by staff within the university for each taxonomy process, aggregated by employee level
  • Percentage of effort captured at each level to calculate estimated FTEs per taxonomy process
  • Analysis supported by review of key operating data and metrics
Fragmentation Analysis
  • Taxonomy process where 50% or less of the FTEs are not centrally located
  • Can be advantageous when local support is required, but problematic when it duplicates effort
Benchmark Set1comparison cohort
  • Peer institutions of comparable enrollment, program mix and public-sector pay structure, screened on four criteria
  • Where a process has no peer coverage, campus performance stands alone — no interpolated peer value is reported

1 Benchmark cohort screened in April 2014; enrollment band ±8,000 FTE.

Source: NMSU HR documentation review and staff interviews (Feb–Apr 2014); Deloitte staffing framework, taxonomy v3.1. Definitions align to the taxonomy on the preceding page.
