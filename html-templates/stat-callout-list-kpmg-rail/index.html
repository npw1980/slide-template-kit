Wellbeing programme has scaled activity, but impact tracking has not kept pace with the promise

Activity counts have grown fourfold since FY21, yet the evidence base for “wellbeing outcomes” remains survey-staffed, not outcome-measured.

  • Programme footprint expanded from 3,100 hours delivered in FY21 to 12,870 hours in FY23, driven by manager-led toolkits and opt-in courses across 246,000 colleagues.
  • Reach is broad but shallow: the global survey captures 1 in 3 employees on wellbeing priorities, and only a minority of member firms report repeat measures against a baseline.
  • Community programmes contributed a further 23.6M hours of wellbeing-adjacent impact in FY23, none of which is reflected in the wellbeing reporting line.
