An example of a strong insurance ecosystem is ERGO Safe Home (ERGO Group AG), a virtual product bundle of home contents insurance, an emergency service and smart-home technology, which cares for customers’ homes when they cannot react to an emergency.2 USAA, which delivers a wide range of services through its associated firms, is a second example of an ecosystem that drives solid engagement with clients.3

Building ecosystems is a vital part of becoming an adaptive “living” business. Doing it effectively relies on application programming interfaces, artificial intelligence and robotic process automation, and on a mindset shift for firms used to working independently. Providers that make the shift are ~10% more likely to upgrade their innovation capability and to develop services that inspire higher customer loyalty.4
