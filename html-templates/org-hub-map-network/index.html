2 | Ecosystem economics

Trust is the product — place it at the centre of a partner network

Providers that own the relationship grow revenue without owning the product.

The advisor position turns one trusted customer relationship into many revenue lines: each node is a partner category where the provider can be the default entry point, and the web between nodes is what makes the bundle feel like one offer rather than a marketplace of parts.1

Non-exhaustive. The question is not how many partners but which few relationships the provider should own — and where the rest should be connected, not owned.
