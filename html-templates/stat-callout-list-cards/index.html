Evidence — customer economics

Retention now turns on personalisation, not price or the breadth of the catalogue

Stated preference

68%

of buyers in our 2023 survey say price is no longer the deciding factor — experience quality is.

Value at stake

2.7×

higher lifetime value among customers who rate their journey as “personalised”.

Downside

$1.4bn

of annual revenue at risk for retailers who cannot personalise beyond the top decile of spend.

So what: the margin to defend is the personalised middle of the base, not the price-sensitive tail — price-led retention spend funds the segment least able to reward it.

Method

3,180 consumers, five markets, Oct–Nov 2023. LTV measured on a 36-month forward window, clipped at churn.

Source: firm consumer demand study, Oct–Nov 2023 (n=3,180); LTV derived from transaction history for the same cohort. Figures are illustrative placeholders.1
