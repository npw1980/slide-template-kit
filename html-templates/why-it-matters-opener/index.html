Electricity demand was already surging from electrification — but gen AI has likely compounded the grid constraints the power sector was already facing.
Hyperscalers expanding their AI footprints push sector consumption into record territory while renewables, storage and demand-side programmes compete for the same interconnection queue. The next four years decide the landing.
1
Grid and interconnection:
Connection queues in Flanders and Wallonia already run 4–7 years; new campuses cannot wait and will default to self-generation and backup gas.
2
Cooling and efficiency:
Liquid-cooled AI halls cut PUE toward 1.10, but the retrofit premium only pencils for operators who consolidate workloads across sites.
3
Sourcing:
PPAs with Belgian offshore wind and French nuclear cover roughly two-thirds of projected 2028 demand; the remainder is still scheduled on the open market.
4
Footprint honesty:
Locational marginal pricing, not national averages, determines the real emissions of each compute hour — and therefore which products clear the disclosure gate.

Source: Deloitte, TMT Predictions 2025 — Belgium edition, ch. GenAI adoption; Elia capacity outlook, Oct 2024; Connected Consumer Survey 2024 (n=1,005).1
