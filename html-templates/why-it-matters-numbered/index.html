2025 Global TMT Predictions · Power & compute
Belgium's data-centre surge is decided by four moves local operators can still make before 2027
1
Build on local compute, not just local hype.
Enterprises should work with Belgian providers to anchor gen AI inference in-country — latency and data-sovereignty rules will matter more than the next model release.
2
Lock the energy contract before the build, not after.
Power purchase and grid-connection agreements signed early decide operating cost for a decade. Waiting for the campus to be complete means bidding against your own deadline.
3
Buy efficiency like a commodity.
Liquid cooling, waste-heat reuse and workload consolidation each carry an internal price. Treat them as procureable items with owners, not as engineering goodwill.
4
Stay small-to-mid and regional where the margin is.
Not everyone should chase hyperscale. Smaller edge footprints near industrial clusters win on latency, grid locality and public goodwill.
Bottom Line
The technology and power industries can jointly absorb the AI energy surge — but only if both build conversations with ecosystem players around what is feasible, legal and viable for business, climate and society.

Source: Deloitte, TMT Predictions 2025 — Belgium edition, ch. Data Centres; Elia System Tool 2025; Eurostat data-centre energy statistics, 2024.
