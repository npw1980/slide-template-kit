  • Demand concentrated in 3–4 states with the largest outlays
Selective, cost driven
  • Acquisition favourable, ownership and maintenance not favourable for private buyers
  • Shared mobility not a favourable option to choose where lanes are absent
  • ICE demand replaced by EV slowly, in 11 of 28 reviewed state policies
Selective, environment conscious
  • Cost economics not favourable outside subsidies
  • Shared mobility not a favourable option
  • Demand driven by environment-conscious and prestige segments
