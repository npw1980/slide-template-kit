Chapter 2 · The ranking

The top twenty own more of the list than last year

Ten years of buybacks and index growth have moved value into a narrower band of companies. Three shifts explain the change and set the shape of the analysis that follows.

  1. 1

    The largest 20 companies hold 51.4% of total market capitalisation, up 2.3pp against March 2022.1

  2. 2

    Technology and financials together account for 46.8% of the list; all other sectors are flat or declining.

  3. 3

    Median organic growth among the top 100 was 6.1%, against 9.9% for the balance of the global universe.2

Source: PwC analysis of company filings and market data, 31 March 2023; market capitalisation in USD, unadjusted, restated for corporate actions.1,2
