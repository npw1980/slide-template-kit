Our commitments — People

Five commitments carry the plan, and each is published with a date and a measure

The commitments are grouped under two themes, each with a named owner and a reporting line into the annual review. Where a target depends on a third party — a supplier, a government, a standards body — that dependency is stated rather than absorbed into the target.a

Purposeful business

  • Always start with a clear purpose — leadership principle, not a slogan
  • Lead the profession in audit quality, with FY22 investment of US$1.0bn
  • Advance inclusion across the workforce, measured by representation at every grade
  • Fund 23,000 hours of pro bono against the 2025 target, reported quarterlyb

Education and communities

  • Support education and training, reaching 2.4m young people by 2030
  • Provide resources to the Global Education Coalition through 2026
  • Aim 10% of philanthropy toward communities where we operate
  • Track every commitment against the published measure, or retire it
