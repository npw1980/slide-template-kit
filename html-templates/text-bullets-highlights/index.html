Underwriting Efficiency
40% of commercial lines underwriters say time is spent on tasks that are not core to the role — three reasons dominate: redundant inputs or manual processes (71%), outdated or inflexible systems (43%), and no information or analytics at the point of need (36%).
Underwriting Quality
Perceived quality of underwriting is at its lowest point since the survey began; only 46% of commercial‑lines underwriters call their frontline practice superior, down 17 pp from 2013 — driven by the same system constraints flagged above.
Investment Priorities
Improving underwriting quality and risk pricing and easing the operating model are the two factors respondents say most improve business outcomes — with 65% of commercial‑lines underwriters reporting their workload has increased without matching technology investment.
Talent & Capability
Firms viewed as superior in talent management for underwriting have dropped by half since 2013 across nearly every performance measure evaluated; training, recruiting and retention planning show the largest single-year declines.
Source: 2021 Accenture P&C Underwriting Survey — commercial-lines cut, n = 295 of 434 total responses
