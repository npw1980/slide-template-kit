Methodology of survey

Background and methodology

To understand how global retail and commercial banks are exploring and implementing GenAI applications, EY-Parthenon teams surveyed 151 banking organizations across 14 markets in July 2023 — a single-field census, reported exactly as collected.a

Population & recruiting

  • Respondents included global, regional and municipal banks within the retail, commercial and SMB segments
  • Regional managers focused on client servicing, marketing, onboarding and technology investment were invited1
  • Screen: decision-making input on GenAI initiatives required

Instrument & calibration

  • Instrument covered strategy, operating model, use cases and budget allocation
  • Question set piloted with three banks before fieldwork opened
  • Free-text responses coded post-field, not during

Analysis & reporting

  • Decision-maker tiers reported, but not restricted to C-suite (heads of product, digital and risk included)
  • Segments cut by deposit base (>$1.0Bn, $500M–1.0Bn, <$500M)2
  • Sub-group cells under n=30 shown as directional only
Limitations — results are not weighted to the population of listed banks and are not projectable to the global market; responses were self-reported against a fixed list of use cases.e
