04 | Lecture material — building the analytics mindset

Three roles, one mindset: analytics works only when consumers, producers and enablers move together1

  • Define the business question before selecting an analytical technique
  • Ask the right questions — three hypothesis frames per engagement
  • Extract, transform and load relevant data — the ETL stage
  • Apply appropriate analytics techniques, from regression to clustering
  • Interpret and share results with stakeholders — one chart, one decision
  • 41% of our analysts work across two of the three roles within year one
