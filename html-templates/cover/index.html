The NBN Effect: Reliable Connectivity Adds $1,9201 a Year in Household Value Across Essential Activities

Two years of survey and conjoint work with 2,420 households1 separates the value households get from the network into two halves: the essential tasks that have to happen online, and the enriching activities people choose.

1 Online survey of nbn™ internet users, fieldwork March 2021 (n = 2,420, weighted to ABS regional distribution); economic value modelled with a discrete-choice conjoint experiment at 95% confidence.
