What Michelle’s story changes about how we fund the whole curriculum

This is the story of Michelle — a first-generation student at a mid-sized state university.

  1. She is the first in her family to attend college, so nobody in her network knows how to file aid paperwork.
  2. She carries a full course load and works twenty hours a week, which makes every dollar of the aid package binding.
  3. For her to graduate on time, advising, transport, and scheduling have to work together — not one at a time.

Source: learner success survey 2026 (n = 4,120) and 26 student interviews; the persona is an anonymized composite, details changed.
