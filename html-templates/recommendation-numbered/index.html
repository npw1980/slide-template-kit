Recommendations  ·  Phase 2 scope
Twelve opportunities carry forward; two need a Phase 2 decision now
Based on 37 interviews, 4 workshops and 12 published assessments, we recommend the following for Phase 2.
2 of 12 are priority items — no Phase 2 start without a decision on these
1 Standardise medtech pathways to pharma parity, removing the 96-day assessment gap seen in 14 of 20 cases.
2 Future-proof precision medicine pathways for 12 rare-disease indications with a standing criteria review.
3 Lightly regulate digital health adoption via a 3-tier classification, not a new licensing regime.
4 Review organisational mandates across the 4 regional centres to close duplication in 3 overlapping boards.
5 Fund innovation and incentives through a ring-fenced pool tied to contracted outcomes, not block grants.1
6 Assess cultural barriers to collaborative working — 218 of 434 respondents named funding as decisive.
7 Define data standards for end-to-end pathway traceability, including consent and linkage rules.2
8 Incentivise NHS involvement in clinical development against a published commercial framework.
9 Update reimbursement methods to incorporate early access and real-world evidence for 4 therapy classes.
10 Promote patient engagement across the end-to-end pathway, with 3 mandated checkpoints per case.
11 Use regional centres as test beds — AHSNs to pilot 2 innovation routes before national scale-up.
12 Publish Phase 2 governance and sequencing so owners and dates are visible outside the programme.
1 Pool size assumes 60 FTE @ $90/hr through 2016-17; excludes capital. 2 Consent model pending legal review; see methodology p103.
Source: 37 stakeholder interviews, 4 workshops and Deecon analysis
