Methodology: four evidence streams, each fielded to a different population in early 2021

1 | Methodology
DCE survey
Source

Discrete Choice Experiment (DCE) fielded to current nbn™ users, with respondents choosing between hypothetical plan bundles.

Information
  • Consumer surplus derived from the nbn™ network by nbn users
  • Sample size: 1,120 users; ten attributes per task
  • Field dates: 08 Mar – 02 Apr 2021
NBN Co
Source

Commercially licensed network-usage extracts provided by NBN Co under data-sharing agreement dated January 2021.

Information
  • Aggregate, de-identified usage, download/upload speeds, peak/off-peak mix, plan price
  • Coverage: 8.3M premises active over the window
  • nbn™ activations, month by month
Accenture survey
Source

Survey of nbn™ users conducted by the Accenture Consumer Pulse panel in February 2021.

Information
  • Importance of and time spent on online activities over the nbn™ network
  • Reliance on key online services during COVID-19
  • Sample: 2,115 responses; 94% response quality after cleaning
Secondary research
Source

Published data from national statistical agencies and open government sources on household connectivity and digital adoption.

Information
  • Benchmarks used to weight both survey streams
  • Reference: ABS 8731.0 household connectivity, Mar 2021 release
