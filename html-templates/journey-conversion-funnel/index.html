14 | Customer journey — awareness to purchase

The journey is lost between consideration and trial, not at awareness1

Reach converts to consideration in line with category; only 27.1% of considerers ever trial — 2.1pp under benchmark, and the only gap worth a plan.

Base: 1,284,000 unique adults aged 18–64 reached by paid and organic media in Q1 2026, single-source panel (n=1,284,000; fieldwork 12 Jan – 28 Mar 2026). The four stages are the same respondents tracked forward, not four samples.

Source: Journey Analytics panel wave 14, stage-by-stage unique-audience reconciliation, Q1 2026; category benchmarks from the 2025 Media Effectiveness Study, 112 campaigns2.
