Executive summary

Prepared for Board of Directors Independent review · Series A findings

Adoption has moved from experimentation to a standing budget line: spend is rising, deployment is spreading into new functions, and the binding constraint is now the workforce — not the technology.

  1. 1

    Investment is accelerating. 83% of executives expect GenAI investment to grow over the next three years, and 78% are confident the planned spend will pay back within the horizon.1

  2. 2

    Deployment is broadening. Within one to three years, 61% plan to expand existing GenAI initiatives and 55% will introduce the technology into business functions that have no deployment today.1

  3. 3

    Workforce readiness is the bottleneck. Only 16% call their workforce highly equipped, even though training programs reached 69%, up from 12% a year earlier — capacity is rising, but behind demand.

  4. 4

    Risk attention has shifted. Cybersecurity and data quality now lead the risk agenda, while regulatory uncertainty fell to 13% from 24% as guidance matured across the survey markets.1

  5. 5

    Revenue impact is real but early. 52% of adopters report GenAI opening new revenue opportunities, concentrated so far in marketing and R&D rather than core operations.
